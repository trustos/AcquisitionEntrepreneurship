## Fit-to-build read

Strong fit. The team can run Swift/SwiftUI, integrate RevenueCat, and own the ASO workflow. The path to more value is product engineering: build the Android version, add AI-powered verse reflection and personalization (obvious LLM integration), add a web/desktop companion, introduce higher-value subscription tiers, and improve ASO keyword coverage. None of that requires a sales team or a content treadmill. Growth-lever gate: ASO (App Store search = organic product discovery) — fits. This is not a buy-your-weakness situation.

## Does the value transfer? (key-person check)

Yes, substantially. The growth engine is App Store organic search (ASO), which is tied to the app's keywords, ratings, and category placement — not the founder's personal audience or relationships. The app name ("Quran Daily") is the brand. RevenueCat subscriber data transfers with the account. The Quran text/translation data source needs to be confirmed (most apps use open-source Quran data — this is likely a non-issue, but must be verified). The main transfer risk is procedural (iOS app transfer + RevenueCat account handover), not structural.

## Seller questions to send (prioritized)

1. **[Revenue / IP — deal-breaker]** Share a RevenueCat or Stripe export covering the full lifetime of the app (May 2025 to present) — all-time cash receipts by month, active subscription count, and plan-mix (weekly vs annual). — *resolves: revenue_evidence → buyer_verified; unit economics*

2. **[Transfer — deal-breaker]** Walk us through the iOS app transfer: does your developer account qualify for an App Store Connect 'Transfer App' action? Are there any active auto-renewable subscriptions that would block or complicate it? What is the plan for the RevenueCat account handover? — *resolves: ip_transferable gate*

3. **[Churn — high priority]** What is monthly subscriber churn? Can you share RevenueCat's subscriber retention chart or a cohort view? — *resolves: pmf_retention; >5%/month changes verdict to PASS*

4. **[Adverse selection — high priority]** Why are you selling after 14 months, and why at $1,500? What would you do to grow this if you were staying? — *resolves: seller_adverse_selection; the single biggest swing factor*

5. **[Revenue history]** What was the app's revenue from May 2025 to May 2026 (before TrustMRR tracking)? Is the $70–77/month run-rate a recent acceleration or has growth been flat since launch? — *resolves: growth_market; pmf_retention*

6. **[Ops and content]** What does running this take weekly — support, updates, ASO maintenance? What is the source of the Quran text and translation data, and does the license permit commercial use and transfer? — *resolves: operability_upside; any non-transferable content license would be a blocker*

7. **[Full asset list]** Confirm everything that transfers: Apple Developer account (or app transfer), RevenueCat account, any social handles, domain, email list, and the Quran text license. Are you open to seller financing, a retention-linked earnout, or a holdback? — *resolves: ip_transferable; value_transfers; deal terms*

8. **[Competition]** You describe Islam as a 'relatively untapped niche,' but Muslim Pro has 30M+ users, Quran.com has a major app, and iQuran/Quran Majeed are established. What specifically differentiates Quran Daily, and what is your current App Store rank for primary keywords? — *resolves: moat_defensibility*

## Recommended next step

**At $1,500, the downside is bounded — but send Q1–Q4 before committing.** Questions 1–2 are hard gates: if RevenueCat export shows flat or declining cash and the app transfer is complicated, walk away. If revenue reconciles from source, the app transfer is clean, and churn is below ~3%/month, this is a low-risk learning acquisition for a team that can add Android, AI features, and better pricing — potentially turning a $900/yr asset into something meaningfully larger.
